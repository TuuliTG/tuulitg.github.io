MealFriends

Cook together, plan together

MealFriends is a recipe app for iPhone and Android. Your household shares one recipe book and one weekly meal plan, and you can see what the friends you follow are cooking.

  • Keep your recipes in one book, with photos, notes, labels and stars.
  • Import a recipe from a web link or from photos of a recipe.
  • Plan the week's dishes together with your household.
  • Share what you cooked with your household or the people who follow you.

MealFriends is in a small, invitation-only test. It has no advertising and no tracking, and your data is stored in the EU.
