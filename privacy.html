MealFriends

Privacy policy

Effective 14 November 2026.

MealFriends is built to keep as little about you as possible. There is no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. Nothing you add leaves your household unless you choose to share it. This policy explains what we store, why, who else handles it, and your rights.

Who is responsible

MealFriends is made by Tuuli Toivanen-Gripentrog, a private individual in Finland, who is the controller of your personal data. Contact: mealfriends.app@gmail.com.

Who can see what

  • The recipe book and week menu are seen only by the members of your household.
  • Your profile is private by default: only your household, people you follow or who follow you, and people who asked to follow you or you asked to follow see your name and handle. Others can only follow your exact handle.
  • A post is seen only by the audience you choose for it: your household, or your household and the followers you accepted. Someone you blocked, or who blocked you, never sees it.
  • A recipe in a post is a copy taken when you post. Your household's stars, labels and comments are never posted, and only the photos you tick are shared.

Importing recipes and smart reading

When you import a recipe from a link, our server (in the EU) fetches that one public page, so the site sees our server rather than you. The page isn't stored. An imported recipe's photo stays on its website, and your phone loads it from there, so that site sees your phone's IP address, as when you open the page in a browser.

Smart reading runs only when you tap it. It sends the text of the public page, or the photos you chose for importing, to Anthropic's AI service, which reads out the recipe. Nothing about you is sent. Anthropic processes it outside the EU, doesn't use it to train its models and keeps it only for a limited time. Photos sent for reading are not stored by us unless you choose to keep them with the recipe.

Services that handle data for us

  • Supabase: database, sign-in and photo storage, in the EU. Supabase keeps short technical logs, including IP addresses, for security and troubleshooting.
  • Google and Apple: sign-in, when you choose them.
  • Anthropic: smart reading, outside the EU (see above).
  • Expo: delivers app updates, outside the EU. When the app opens, it asks Expo for a newer version. This sends the app version and a random ID made on your phone, and Expo sees your IP address. Nothing about you or your recipes is sent.

Each works under a data processing agreement with us. Transfers outside the EU rely on the European Commission's standard contractual clauses or the EU–US Data Privacy Framework, as each agreement provides. We don't sell your data or share it with anyone else, except where the law requires it.

How long we keep it

  • As long as you have an account, unless you delete things sooner.
  • When you delete your account, your profile, posts and their photos, follows, blocks and stars are deleted. Recipes, comments and dishes you added stay in your household's book and plan for the other members, without your name. If you are the household's last member, the household is deleted with everything in it.
  • Copies of your recipes that other people added to their own books keep the recipe but show "a deleted user".
  • Backups of the database are kept for a short time and then overwritten.

Your rights

You can:

  • see and download your data in the app: Me → Account → Export my data;
  • correct your profile, recipes and posts in the app;
  • delete your account and data in the app: Me → Account → Delete account. This also removes MealFriends' access to your Google or Apple account;
  • object to or ask us to restrict processing, or ask any question, by emailing us.

We answer within a month. You can also complain to the Finnish Data Protection Ombudsman (tietosuoja.fi) or the authority in your own EU country.

Children

MealFriends is for people aged 16 and over. Please don't post pictures that show children's faces. If you believe a child under 16 uses MealFriends, tell us and we will delete the account.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we tell you in the app before the change takes effect. The date at the top shows the current version.
